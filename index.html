QMSS Master Thesis: Data Deterioration & Democratic Erosion

Statistical Casualties of Autocratization: Mapping Data Deterioration and SDG Progress (January - August 2025)
Designed study that examines causal pathways between democratic backsliding, statistical capacity deterioration, and SDG performance across 170+ countries (2016-2023). Implemented advanced econometric techniques in R including dynamic two-way fixed effects (TWFE) and first-difference models to isolate immediate and incremental effects while controlling for unobserved confounders.
